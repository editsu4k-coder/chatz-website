FAQ

Quick answers.

All questions
What is ChatZ?

A friend-only Android app that shares a summary of your digital day — screen time, top apps, data usage and activity status — with a circle of friends you personally accepted. There is no feed, no followers, no advertising, and no third-party analytics SDKs.

What do my friends see about me?

Only what you allow in Profile → Privacy, and only today's summary — never your bio, date of birth or education. A category you switch off isn't shown at all (no “0”, no old value — it's simply absent). In Silent mode, friends see “Stats sharing is paused” instead of any numbers.

Is ChatZ free?

Yes — ChatZ is free to download and use. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Why does ChatZ request screen-time (Usage) access?

Real screen-time numbers can only be read with Android's Usage access permission. ChatZ uses it solely to build your daily summary on your device — raw app lists, screenshots, browser history, message contents and credentials are never uploaded.
