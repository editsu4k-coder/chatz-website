Self-serve fixes

Screen-time access blocked or greyed out?

Android 13+ blocks sensitive permissions — like Usage access — for apps installed outside the Play Store. The toggle shows a “Restricted setting” warning. This is Android being careful with sideloaded apps, not an error caused by ChatZ. You unlock it once per device:

  1. Open ChatZ's App info (long-press the ChatZ icon → App info).
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top-right — it may be called “More” on some phones (Oppo/ColorOS, realme).
  3. Choose “Allow restricted settings” and confirm with your PIN, pattern or fingerprint.
  4. Come back and toggle Usage access ON — ChatZ re-checks automatically.

ChatZ always shows the real permission state and never fakes a grant — if Usage access is off, you'll see an honest “no access” state instead of made-up numbers.

Stats missing or out of date

Two usual causes:

  1. Usage access was revoked — ChatZ shows a permission screen; re-grant it from there.
  2. Android deferred the background sync — WorkManager runs about every 15 minutes, but battery saver or aggressive OEMs can delay it. Opening the app refreshes immediately.

Keep background sync reliable

For stats that update even when ChatZ is closed, keep ChatZ exempt from battery optimization: Me → Settings → Permissions → Background activity. Each row there opens the exact Android settings screen, and status re-checks automatically when you come back.

Notifications not arriving

Check your in-app notification preferences in the profile, and make sure Android notifications for ChatZ are on (Me → Settings → Permissions shows the live state). Background delivery depends on Google Play services; ChatZ also shows unread dots in the app, so you never miss anything important.
