Getting started

What is ChatZ?

ChatZ is a friend-only Android app that shares a summary of your digital day — screen time, top apps, data usage and activity status — with a circle of friends you personally accepted. There is no public feed, no follower system, no advertising, and no third-party analytics SDKs.

Is ChatZ free?

Yes. ChatZ is free to download and use — no ads, no in-app purchases, no premium tier.

Does ChatZ work on Android?

ChatZ is built for Android and runs on Android 6.0 and newer. It's distributed as a direct APK download (not through the Play Store), so you install it yourself — see the install steps.

How do I sign in?

ChatZ uses Google Sign-In only. Tap Continue with Google on the welcome screen — the account chooser opens. If nothing happens, check that you're online and that Google Play services is up to date. The first sign-in takes you through profile setup.

How do I set up or change my profile?

First sign-in walks you through your name, a unique @handle, an avatar and a daily goal. To change anything later, open the Me tab — name, avatar, bio and socials live there. Friends find you by your @handle.

Sharing & privacy

What information does ChatZ share?

Only today's device summary — screen time (total and per app), data usage, battery state and whether you're on Wi-Fi or mobile data — plus your presence (online / last seen) and your streak. Never photos, files, location, contacts, camera, microphone, notification contents, other apps' messages, or browser history.

What do my friends see about me?

Only what you allow in Profile → Privacy, and only today's summary — never your bio, date of birth or education. A category you switch off isn't shown to friends at all (no “0”, no old value — it's simply absent), and it's removed from your stored snapshot right away. In Silent mode, friends see “Stats sharing is paused” instead of any numbers.

Can I control what friends see?

Yes, per category. Screen time, app usage, data usage and active status each have their own switch in Profile → Privacy, and the Active / Silent mode switch pauses everything at once. Enforcement is server-side: what you turn off can't be read by anyone, not just hidden in the app's interface. Try the interactive demo on the home page.

What's the difference between Active and Silent mode?

The mode control is on your Home dashboard. Active (the default) shares today's stats with your friends according to your individual category switches — Active never overrides a category you've switched off. Silent immediately stops all stat sharing: friends see a “Stats sharing is paused” notice instead of your numbers, and you appear offline while Silent is on. Switching back to Active restores exactly what you had switched on — Silent never changes your category settings.

Can people who aren't my friends see my stats?

No. Usage stats and presence are readable only by you and by mutual friends — enforced by Firebase security rules. Blocked accounts can't see your profile, stats or online status, and can't message or re-request you while blocked.

Installation & updates

How do I install the APK?
  1. Tap Download for Android and open the downloaded APK.
  2. Android will ask to allow installs from your browser or file manager — allow it for this install.
  3. Tap Install, then open ChatZ and sign in with Google.

Only download ChatZ from this website or the official GitHub Releases page — the download is SHA-256 checksummed so you can verify it.

Why does ChatZ request Screen Time / Usage Access?

Real screen-time numbers can only be read with Android's Usage access permission — it's a special access grant, not a regular permission. ChatZ uses it solely to build your daily summary on your device; raw per-app lists, screenshots, browser history, messages and credentials are never uploaded. Without it, ChatZ shows an honest “no access” state instead of fake numbers.

Why might Android ask me to allow restricted settings?

Android 13+ blocks sensitive permissions — like Usage access — for apps installed outside the Play Store, so the toggle can look greyed out with a “Restricted setting” warning. That's Android being careful with sideloaded apps, not a problem with ChatZ. You unlock it once per device: App info → ⋮ menu → Allow restricted settings, confirm with your PIN or fingerprint, then toggle Usage access on. Full walkthrough.

How are updates delivered?

ChatZ checks for updates automatically and shows a card when a new version is available — new builds ship on the GitHub Releases page, and the app verifies each download's SHA-256 checksum before installing. You can always grab the latest APK from this website too.

ChatZ won't install — what now?

Make sure the download completed (the APK is around 9 MB), that “Install unknown apps” is allowed for the app you opened the APK with, and that you have enough free storage. If an older ChatZ is installed with a lower version code, the new one installs straight over it and keeps your data.

Friends & social

How do I add a friend?

Friends tab → Find people → search their @handle or name → tap Add. They accept from their Friends tab, and then you can see each other's shared screen-time stats. Requests also appear as in-app notifications.

How do reactions and comments work?

Open a friend from the Friends tab and react or comment on their screen-time card. They get an in-app notification, and you can reply in threads on the card. Reactions and comments expire after 24 hours — today is today.

How do messages work?

Tap the message icon on a friend's card in the Friends tab. Messages are delivered through your account and a dot on the Friends tab marks unread conversations.

What does “last seen” mean?

It shows when a friend was last active in ChatZ — updated while the app is open and marked away shortly after it closes. While Silent mode is on you appear offline: presence stops updating and friends see you as away, just like when the app is closed.

What is the 🔥 streak number?

It counts the consecutive days you've opened ChatZ — one more each day you come back, reset to 1 after a missed day. Your streak sits on your Home card and friends can see it on your card too. It's just a habit badge: no data behind it beyond the day counter itself.

How do blocking and unfriending work?

Open someone's profile (or their card in the Friends tab) and use the menu in the top-right. Unfriend removes the friendship for both of you right away; they get a notification. Block additionally stops them from messaging you, seeing your profile, stats or online status, or sending you friend requests — any friendship and pending requests are removed. Blocking doesn't report them. Unblocking lets them see your shared stats and send a new request again, but it does not restore the friendship automatically.

How do I report someone?

Open their profile → menu (top-right) → Report, pick a reason and optionally add details. Reports are private — the person is never told, and they can never read the report or see who sent it. Reporting doesn't block or unfriend anyone; if you also want to stop them, block them separately.

Troubleshooting & account

Why are my stats missing or out of date?

Two usual causes: Usage access was revoked (ChatZ shows a permission screen — re-grant it from there), or Android deferred the background sync. Opening the app refreshes immediately; for reliable background syncing, keep ChatZ exempt from battery optimization (Settings → Permissions → Background activity).

Why didn't I get a notification?

Check in-app notification preferences in your profile, and make sure Android notifications for ChatZ are on — Me → Settings → Permissions shows the live state. Note that background alerts depend on Google Play services delivering them; ChatZ also shows unread dots in the app.

How do I sign out or delete my account?

Sign out from the Me tab — that removes your session from this device. There's no in-app delete button yet; email ChatZ Support to delete your account and data.
